# ADR-NNNN: Short noun phrase naming the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Partially superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed, if known (omit otherwise)
- **Supersedes:** ADR-NNNN, stating whether in full or in part and what still holds (omit if none)

## Context

The forces at play, stated as facts: the problem that forces a decision now, the ranked quality attributes, and the constraints (technical, team, budget, deadlines, compliance). Mark anything unverified as an assumption.

## Options considered

The options someone could really choose, and in one or two sentences why each was or wasn't taken.

## Decision

We will ...

Only what was actually decided. If you are recording a decision someone else made, put details you are suggesting under "Pending" at the end of this record.

## Consequences

What becomes true after this decision: what gets easier, what gets harder, new risks and operational costs, and any follow-up work.

## Revisit when

The signal or threshold that should reopen this decision.
